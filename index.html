About GMC

A modern, spacious hospital built for the people of Gujranwala

Gujranwala Medical Complex (GMC) is a women-owned, multi-specialty hospital located on Pindi Bypass (Lohianwala Bypass), GT Road. Our goal is simple: to make quality healthcare accessible, reliable and complete – without the stress of travelling to Lahore.

From emergency care and surgery to mother & child health, our consultants, nurses and staff work together to give every patient compassionate, professional treatment.

  • Clean wards & private rooms
  • Modern operation theatres
  • NICU & baby care
  • In-house X-ray & diagnostics
  • OPD, IPD & pharmacy
  • Courteous, caring staff

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is Gujranwala Medical Complex located?

Near Zoom Petrol Pump, Pindi Bypass (Lohianwala Bypass), GT Road, Gujranwala, Punjab, Pakistan.

Is the emergency department open 24 hours?

Yes. Our emergency department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public holidays.

How can I book an appointment?

Call 055-3416004, 055-3416005 or 0333-1187141, or use the online appointment form to send your request on WhatsApp.

Which specialist doctors are available?

We have consultants in general & laparoscopic surgery, gynecology, pediatrics, neurosurgery, cardiology, gastroenterology, urology, ENT, eye surgery, plastic surgery, rheumatology, psychiatry, psychology and dentistry.

Is the hospital wheelchair accessible?

Yes. GMC has a wheelchair accessible entrance, parking, restroom and seating.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. We accept cash, credit cards and debit cards.
