Radiology & Laboratory at Gujranwala Medical Complex

Fast, accurate diagnosis decides treatment. Gujranwala Medical Complex has an in-house digital X-ray room and a clinical laboratory, so patients get tests and reports without visiting multiple places – and emergency doctors get answers within minutes when it matters.

Our laboratory performs routine blood tests, hepatitis screening, sugar profiles, and pre-operative workups; digital radiography means lower radiation dose and instantly viewable images for your doctor.

Conditions we treat

  • Routine blood tests (CBC, sugar, cholesterol)
  • Hepatitis B and C screening
  • Typhoid and dengue testing
  • Urine tests
  • Pre-operative workups
  • Fracture and chest X-rays
  • Pregnancy-related blood tests
  • Thyroid and liver function tests

Services & procedures

FAQ

Radiology & Laboratory – frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment for lab tests?

No appointment is needed for most blood and urine tests – walk in during laboratory hours. Some tests require fasting, which our staff will explain on the phone if you call 055-3416004.

How soon are reports ready?

Routine blood tests are usually ready the same day; specialized tests may take longer. Digital X-ray images are available immediately for your doctor to review.

Is digital X-ray safer than old X-ray?

Digital radiography uses a lower radiation dose than older film systems and produces instantly viewable images, so repeat exposures due to technical errors are rare.
